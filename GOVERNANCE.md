# Governance

## Decision-Making Model

Jasonette Reborn uses a **BDFL (Benevolent Dictator For Life)** model. The project lead has final authority on all technical and community decisions.

## Current Lead

**Thomas** — project lead and primary maintainer.

## Succession

If the project lead is inactive for **60 consecutive days** (no commits, no issue/PR responses, no public communication), the following succession process activates:

1. Any maintainer may open a "Succession Notice" issue.
2. A 14-day waiting period allows the lead to respond and resume activity.
3. If no response, maintainers with merge access vote to appoint a new lead (simple majority).
4. The new lead assumes all responsibilities and access.

## Path to Maintainership

1. **Contributor** — anyone who submits a merged PR.
2. **Trusted Contributor** — consistent quality contributions over 3+ months. May be granted triage access.
3. **Maintainer** — trusted contributors invited by the project lead. Maintainers have merge access and participate in succession votes.

## Decision Thresholds

| Decision | Required |
|---|---|
| Bug fix merge | 1 maintainer approval |
| Feature merge | Lead approval |
| Spec change | Lead approval + 7-day comment period |
| Breaking change | Lead approval + 14-day comment period |
| New maintainer | Lead invitation |
